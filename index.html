FAQ

Can I use copyrighted characters like Tom and Jerry?

No. TojeToons only supports original characters you create or upload. Using copyrighted characters risks demonetization and channel strikes.

How many videos can I generate?

It depends on your plan — 5 on the free trial, 30 on Pro, 100 on Business.

Do I need to know video editing?

Not at all. The AI handles rendering, voiceover, and editing. You just approve the script.

What languages are supported?

Telugu voiceover and scripts are fully supported, with more languages coming.
